Week 6: Design Research Lab

October 9, 2026

This week, we worked on design research, using a hypothetical about a bakery deciding whether or not to extend its hours. We were tasked with developing interview questions for various employees and customers, to determine whether or not it would be worth it to invest in the extension. This exercise required adaptability and being able to understand how to develop a set of questions catered to each person. Not only did our questions have to be relevant to the role we were interviewing, but we also had to make sure they would be effective in helping us determine our answer. I feel like the biggest challenge was looking ahead to the kinds of information we were looking for, and developing questions that would reveal that.
